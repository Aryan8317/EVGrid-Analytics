
# Analysis Guide

## 1. Business Understanding
Goal: identify demand patterns, station performance and capacity constraints.

## 2. Data Cleaning
Check missing values, duplicates, cancelled sessions and numerical outliers.

## 3. KPI Layer
- Total sessions
- Total energy
- Total revenue
- Average charging duration
- Revenue per session
- Station utilization proxy
- Peak-hour demand

## 4. EDA
Study demand by:
- Hour
- Day of week
- Month
- Station
- Customer segment

## 5. Business Recommendations
Translate patterns into operational decisions:
- Add capacity where peak utilization is consistently high.
- Investigate low-utilization stations.
- Consider time-based pricing.
- Plan maintenance outside peak demand windows.
